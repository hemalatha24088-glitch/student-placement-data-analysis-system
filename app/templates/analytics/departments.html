Analytical Formula Reference & Methodology

  • Department Placement Rate: Calculated strictly using the department's own eligible denominator:
    Rate (%) = (Distinct Placed Eligible Students / Total Department Eligible Students) × 100.
  • Unplaced Students: Unplaced = Eligible Students − Distinct Placed Students. Bounded to ensure non-negative counts.
  • Multiple Offers Handling: Candidates securing multiple offers are counted exactly once in the placed count numerator.
